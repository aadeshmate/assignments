Flute
The flute is a woodwind instrument that produces sound when the player blows air across an opening, called the embouchure hole. It has no reed.
Types: The concert flute (Western) is made of metal, usually silver or nickel. The bansuri is an Indian bamboo flute widely used in Hindustani classical music.
Range: The concert flute covers about three octaves, starting from middle C (C4).
Playing: Pitch is changed by covering and uncovering holes or pressing keys, and by changing air speed and lip position.
Use: Popular in orchestras, folk music, film music and Indian classical and devotional music.
Beginner tip: Start with long tones to build a steady breath and a clear tone.
