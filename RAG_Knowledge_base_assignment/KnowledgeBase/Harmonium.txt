Harmonium
The harmonium is a free-reed keyboard instrument. Air is pumped through bellows and makes metal reeds vibrate to create sound.
Origin: Invented in Europe in the 19th century and adopted widely in India, where it became central to bhajan, kirtan, qawwali and classical vocal accompaniment.
Playing: The player pumps the bellows with one hand (or a foot pedal in some models) and plays the keys with the other hand. Stops and drone controls change the tone.
Types: Hand-pumped (Indian style) and foot-pumped (European style).
Range: Usually 3 to 3.5 octaves, often with a scale-changer to shift the key.
Beginner tip: Keep the air pressure steady for even volume while playing.
