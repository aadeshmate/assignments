Piano
The piano is a keyboard instrument where pressing a key makes a felt hammer strike strings inside, producing sound.
Keys: A standard piano has 88 keys, 52 white and 36 black, covering over seven octaves (A0 to C8).
Types: Acoustic grand, acoustic upright, and digital pianos or keyboards.
Pedals: Usually three: sustain (right), sostenuto (middle) and soft (left).
Playing: Both hands play different parts, typically the right hand melody and the left hand harmony or bass.
Use: Classical, jazz, pop, film music and composition.
Beginner tip: Learn the note layout around middle C and practice scales with correct finger numbering.
