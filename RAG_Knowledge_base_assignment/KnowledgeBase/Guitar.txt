Guitar
The guitar is a stringed instrument played by plucking or strumming strings that run over a fretted neck.
Types: Acoustic (nylon or steel strings), electric (needs an amplifier) and bass guitar (usually 4 strings).
Strings: A standard guitar has 6 strings tuned E A D G B E from lowest to highest.
Playing: Notes are changed by pressing strings against frets. Common techniques include chords, strumming, fingerpicking and bending.
Use: Found in rock, pop, blues, jazz, folk, classical and Bollywood music.
Beginner tip: Learn basic open chords like C, G, D, Em and Am first, and practice changing between them smoothly.
